# 19 Data Governance

## Principles

- Keep source records separate from interpretations.
- Track provenance for imported data.
- Protect private or sensitive fields.
- Prefer open licenses for public datasets.
- Treat public portal records as curated views over source tables; do not publish records unless visibility services mark them public-safe.
- Keep citizen report moderation state and activities separate from project, procurement, document, and analytics source facts.

## V2 Expansion Notes

V2 adds immutable acquisition records, source classes, publisher attribution, license/rights decisions, checksums, supersession links, extraction runs, evidence spans, blind review decisions, subject responses, and versioned public projections. Government and non-governmental sources remain distinguishable. Cross-source agreement is recorded as evidence, never inferred as legal truth.

Precise browser coordinates are not retained. Identity trust uses verified email, optional two-factor authentication, and account history; CivicLens does not collect NID documents, NID numbers, NID-derived metadata, or NID hashes.
