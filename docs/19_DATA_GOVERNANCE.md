# 19 Data Governance

## Principles

- Keep source records separate from interpretations.
- Track provenance for imported data.
- Protect private or sensitive fields.
- Prefer open licenses for public datasets.
- Treat public portal records as curated views over source tables; do not publish records unless visibility services mark them public-safe.
- Keep citizen report moderation state and activities separate from project, procurement, document, and analytics source facts.
