# Project Overview

CivicLens is a civic data platform that helps people understand public-sector projects, budgets, procurements, documents, and status reports.

## Vision

Create a trustworthy, searchable, and explainable platform for civic information. The platform should support evidence-based public decision-making without making unsupported legal accusations.

## Primary Users

- Citizens who want transparent project information.
- Government staff who maintain project records.
- Journalists and researchers who analyze public data.
- Administrators who review submissions and manage data quality.

## V1 Scope

V1 delivers the operational foundation: Laravel app, relational database, roles, project records, budgets, procurements, documents, search, dashboards, and audit logs.

## V2 Expansion Notes

V2 should add public APIs, OCR, semantic search, geospatial visualization, anomaly indicators, and stronger deployment/observability.
